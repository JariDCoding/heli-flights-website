Good to know, before you book

Location & timing

  • Departure from our own heliport, about 10 minutes by car from the festival grounds (Reet area).
  • The exact address follows in your booking confirmation.
  • Arrive at least 15 minutes before your time slot.

Safety

  • Max. 120 kg per person, for the weight and balance of the aircraft.
  • All passengers follow the safety instructions of operator Heli One.
  • The pilot always has the final say on every flight.

Weather & refunds

  • If your flight cannot take place (bad weather, unsafe conditions), you rebook for free or get a full refund.
  • The pilot decides the final go/no-go based on the conditions.
  • We notify you as early as possible about weather-related changes.
